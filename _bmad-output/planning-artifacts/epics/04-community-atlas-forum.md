# EPIC-04: THE ATLAS (COMMUNITY & FORUM)

## Summary
Implement the anonymized price atlas and context-aware community forum.

## User Stories
- **STORY-04-01**: As an user, I want to see a regional price atlas for materials to optimize my purchases.
- **STORY-04-02**: As an user, I want an anonymized forum to discuss professional tips with peers.
- **STORY-04-03**: As an user, I want the AI to route my technical questions to owners of the same product.
- **STORY-04-04**: As an user, I want granular control over my contribution status (Community Opt-in/out).
